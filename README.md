## 🪙 Coin-Toss Game Rules & Mechanics

1. **Mission**: Flip the coin and land **3 heads in a row**.
2. **Streak Progression**:
   - 🪙 **1st Head**: *"Nice! It's HEADS! You're off to a great start!"* (Token 1 lights up with golden aura + chime).
   - 🪙 **2nd Head**: *"Another HEADS! You're one step away!"* (Token 2 illuminates + ascending melody).
   - 🪙 **3rd Head (JACKPOT)**: *"JACKPOT! 3 IN A ROW! Welcome to Harsh's Universe!"* (Token 3 pops, triumphant fanfare plays, confetti explodes across the screen, and the developer universe unlocks!).
   - ❌ **Tails**: Streak resets to 0. Dynamic feedback encourages the visitor to try again.
3. **Recruiter Fast-Pass**: A dedicated instant-unlock button is included for reviewers and recruiters who wish to jump directly into the full developer profile.
